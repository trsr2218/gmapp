Suzuki Alto K10

The Alto K10 is a small car with a big personality. It features a lightweight yet powerful 1,000cc K-Next engine, available in manual and automatic transmission, and delivers approximately 20 km per litre, one of the most fuel-efficient options in its class in Zambia. Comfortable, spacious for its footprint, and easy to live with in city traffic.
