Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask

Can't find what you're after? Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form.

Where are Guardian Motors showrooms located?
We have two showrooms: our head office in Lusaka, on the corner of Kabelenga Road and Tuleteka Road, and our Ndola branch, trading as Sundat Motor Sales, on the corner of Broadway and Shinde Street.
Can I buy a car on Nkongole (instalments)?
Yes. Guardian Motors offers Suzuki vehicles on Nkongole financing terms. Speak to our Lusaka sales desk for current requirements, deposit amounts and monthly instalment options.
Are your spare parts genuine?
Every part we sell and fit is 100% genuine Suzuki or Maruti Suzuki stock, sourced directly through the official distributor network, never aftermarket substitutes.
How do I book a service appointment?
Use the Book a Service form, call our Lusaka switchboard on 0211 228 778, or WhatsApp us on +260 777 407 110. Our service desk will confirm your slot by phone or email.
Do you service vehicles other than Suzuki?
Our Lusaka workshop also provides fleet servicing for Nissan vehicles, alongside full servicing for the entire Suzuki car and motorcycle range.
What are your opening hours?
Both showrooms are open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm, and Saturday, 8:00am to 1:00pm. Our Lusaka Parts & Service counter opens slightly earlier, from 7:30am on weekdays.
How do I order a part if I don't know the part number?
No problem. Browse the Genuine Parts catalogue by category, add what you need to your enquiry list, or tell us your vehicle's chassis and engine number and describe the part. Our parts desk will identify and quote the correct genuine component.
Which brands does Guardian Motors distribute?
We are the authorised Zambian distributor for Suzuki Motor Corporation and Maruti Suzuki India Ltd, covering passenger cars, SUVs, commercial vehicles and motorcycles.
How long has Guardian Motors been in business?
Guardian Motors Limited was established in 1969, over 55 years as a proudly Zambian-owned motor company.
Does servicing at Guardian Motors affect my warranty?
No. Servicing with genuine parts at an authorised Guardian Motors workshop keeps your Suzuki manufacturer warranty fully valid.
Can I save vehicles to look at later?
Yes. Tap the heart icon on any vehicle to save it to your wishlist, or "Add to Enquiry" to build a list to send our sales team in one message. Find both on the enquiry cart page.
